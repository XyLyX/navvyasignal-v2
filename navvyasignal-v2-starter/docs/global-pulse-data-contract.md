# Global Clock and Markets Pulse — V2 preview

The six clocks are client-side, use IANA time zones (Dubai, London, New York, New Delhi, Singapore and Tokyo), refresh every 30 seconds and automatically respect daylight saving. The static-export HTML initially shows placeholders to prevent server/client hydration mismatches.

The original Framer top bar was confirmed by the site owner to contain five TradingView Single Quote widgets, each loading `https://s3.tradingview.com/external-embedding/embed-widget-single-quote.js`. V2 restores the exact instruments: `AMEX:SPY`, `BINANCE:BTCUSDT`, `TVC:UKOIL`, `TVC:GOLD` and `BSE:SENSEX`. The sixth Framer iframe is the editor bar and is intentionally excluded.

The quotes are third-party client-side embeds, independent of Notion and Framer content. No API key, proxy, paid feed or custom market-data pipeline is required for these widgets. Exchange delays, access and widget display are governed by TradingView and the relevant exchanges; **do not describe these as guaranteed real-time**. A disclosure is visible below the widgets. No quote values are generated by NavvyaSignal.

Preview verification must confirm all five embeds load in the browser, the layout works on desktop/mobile, third-party scripts are not blocked by site CSP or ad blockers, and the clocks remain correct around DST. If embeds fail, the page must not display fabricated values. The live Framer V1, DNS, Notion records and production automation remain untouched.
